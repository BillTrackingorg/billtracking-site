Delete some of your data

You can delete parts of your data and keep your account.

Signed in, in the app or on this website:

  • Votes: tap your vote again to withdraw it, or withdraw them all at once.
  • Followed bills: tap the bell to unfollow.
  • Keywords: remove them in Settings.
  • Notifications: delete them one at a time, or clear them all. They are also deleted automatically after 30 days.

Each is deleted right away, and leaves our backups within 90 days. Your votes leave the live counts at once. A count already posted or downloaded is not rewritten.

Signed in, Your votes lists every vote you have cast.

For anything else, email contact@billtracking.org from the address you signed in with. We reply within 30 days.

To delete everything, delete your account.
