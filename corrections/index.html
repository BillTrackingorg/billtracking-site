Corrections

1 correction, newest first. A correction never edits or deletes what we published: the archived text of every post stays exactly as it went out, and the correction is applied on top of it — on the card, on the post's own page, and on the bill's.

  • H.Res. 179US tracker

    First sponsor changed

    Corrected 11 September 2026. We misread the record. On 2 September the House agreed, without objection, to a change of the resolution's first sponsor. Our classifier read that as the resolution itself being agreed to. It was not. H.Res. 179 remains in committee — originally "✅🏛️ Agreed to (unanimous consent)" — What's next originally read "Policy adoption (resolutions don't become law)". Source.
