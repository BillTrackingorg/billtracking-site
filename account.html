Sign in

An account does two things: it makes your vote count once, and it keeps the bills you follow the same on every device. Reading never needs one, and never will.

What an account is for

Votes are the one number on this site that is ours rather than an institution's, so they are tied to an account and counted once. Everything else you can see here you can see signed out.

Signed in as

If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your address, this is the relay address Apple gives us. We never see the real one, and we do not need it.

Where your vote is counted from

Votes are published as a breakdown by place. Set this once — it applies wherever you are signed in. Leave it unset and your vote still counts; it simply sits outside the breakdown.
