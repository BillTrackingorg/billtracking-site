S. 434

Space Commerce Advisory Committee Act

Commerce, Science, and Transportation Committee · Sen. Gary Peters (D-MI)

The bill would establish an advisory committee under the Office of Space Commerce to provide recommendations on the commercial space industry, including ways to support its growth and innovation. The committee would identify challenges facing U.S. commercial space companies and review best practices for protecting the Moon and other celestial bodies from contamination, as well as preventing environmental damage to Earth from space activities.[AI-Generated]
