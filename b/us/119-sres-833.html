S.Res. 833

A resolution recognizing the 1960 University of Missouri Tigers Football Team for its undefeated regular season, Orange Bowl victory, and claim to the 1960 National Championship.

Sen. Eric Schmitt (R-MO)

The resolution would honor the 1960 University of Missouri Tigers football team for completing an undefeated regular season, winning the Orange Bowl, and claiming the national championship that year.[AI-Generated]
