H.R. 2069

Stop Secret Spending Act of 2025

Oversight and Government Reform Committee · Rep. Barry Moore (R-AL)

The bill would require federal agencies to publicly report spending on "other transaction agreements" (a type of contract that currently operates with less transparency and fewer regulations than standard government contracts) to the USAspending.gov website, where federal spending is tracked. It would also require the Treasury Department to publish annual reports showing how much federal spending data remains unreported and why, and would direct inspector generals at certain agencies to assess their spending data quality for the next decade.[AI-Generated]
